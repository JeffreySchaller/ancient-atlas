# Gemini Design Brief — "The Last Animals of the Ice Age"

The Ancient Atlas megafauna line. Refined minimal line illustration of
end-Pleistocene megafauna, built on a sacred-geometry armature. One
visual system, scaled across a small product set, expanded over time.
Gerald holds final veto on every design.

## The positioning (the Apple fine line)
Beautiful first, meaningful underneath. A stranger who knows nothing of
the symbolism should want to wear it on looks alone. A person who knows
the story should feel it was made for them. We refuse both failure modes
we see in creator merch:
- **Lacks sophistication** (juvenile cartoon, busy, trend-chasing) — we
  beat this with geometric rigor and restraint.
- **Snobbery** (fussy, exclusionary, cold) — we beat this with warmth,
  charm, and instant readability.

The register is Apple-grade: simple, confident, warm, quietly precise.
Charley Harper's reduction meets Apple's iconographic discipline. Charm
lives in proportion and posture, never in cartoon exaggeration.

**Rams filter (apply to every candidate):** Is there anything left to
remove? Is the charm honest or are we dressing it up? Does the geometry
do the work, or are we decorating? Will it read as well in five years as
today?

## The story (carries into all copy)
These are the last animals of the last Ice Age. Most vanished around
11,700 years ago as the glaciers retreated, the same deglaciation and
sea-level rise that drowned ancient coastlines and the sites we map
(the Sicilian Channel monolith now sits 40 m underwater for the same
reason). Humans saw every one of these animals and painted them on cave
walls. Mammoths still walked Wrangel Island while the first stones of
the Great Pyramid were laid. We are continuing the oldest art tradition
on Earth, with a sharper pencil.

## The brand atom — the point of light (LOCKED 2026-06-13)
The eye is not a detail; it is the brand's smallest unit. It is a single
solid **amber dot (#E8B960)** — the same mark used for a site pin on the
map and the center of the compass. One atom, three scales: a star in the
sky, a pin on the map, an eye in the creature (as above, so below, so
within). Every species inherits the identical dot.
- Shape: a perfect circle. Never a teardrop, leaf, highlight, or iris.
- Size: about 1/9 of head width.
- Placement: high and forward, where the head fur meets the face, on the
  construction geometry.

## Fractal principle — self-similar in structure, minimal in surface
The system is fractal by design: one seed (the point of light) repeats
self-similarly across every scale (compass center · map pin · creature
eye; and the atlas itself nests sites within regions within the whole).
Lean into nature's real fractals when choosing forms — logarithmic-spiral
tusks and horns, dendritic antlers, tessellated shells — but render them
at their cleanest, most reduced line.
**Guardrail:** the complexity lives in the architecture, never in the
surface. NO fractal/Mandelbrot/Apophysis textures, no psychedelic iris,
no ornamental detail — that is the 'lacks sophistication' failure mode.
Be fractal in how the system nests; be Rams-minimal in every single mark.

## Recursive-eye hero piece (ident / master poster ONLY)
Reserve the literal recursion for one signature moment, never the
products: push into the mammoth's amber eye and find the atlas itself, a
field of points of light, among which are creatures, one of whose eyes
opens again into the atlas. Infinite, self-similar, the brand recognizing
itself. This carries the whole fractal idea as motion and story while
every shirt, sticker, and cap stays clean.

## MASTER PROMPT (paste first)

You are an elite icon and illustration designer for a premium brand. The
register is Apple-grade industrial design applied to illustration:
maximum simplicity, geometric precision, quiet warmth. Think Charley
Harper's reductive wildlife crossed with the construction discipline of
Apple's iconography. Confirm you understand, then wait for the subject.

Style system, never break these:
- A single animal, centered, profile or gentle 3/4, on a plain
  background. No scene, no ground line, no text, no watermark, no
  mannequin, no garment.
- Built from the fewest possible confident lines. Forms derived from
  circles, arcs, and golden-ratio proportion — clean geometric
  construction underneath, even though the final lines look effortless.
- Controlled line weight (one primary weight, optional one accent
  weight). Flat color fills only, no gradients, no texture, no shadows,
  no 3D, no painterly rendering.
- Exactly 4 brand colors: near-black obsidian #0D0D12 (ground),
  champagne gold #C9A84C, warm amber #E8B960, soft ivory #F0EEE9.
  Default to a single-animal motif in champagne/ivory line with at most
  one amber accent fill. Must also read on an ivory ground (inverted).
- Charm comes ONLY from proportion and posture: a slightly larger head,
  a calm settled stance (the eye is ALWAYS a single solid amber dot,
  #E8B960, the brand point of light — never a teardrop or detailed iris).
  NEVER from cartoon exaggeration, big-eyed cuteness, blush marks,
  smiles, or sticker-style outlines.
- Refined, not childish. Beautiful to someone who doesn't know the
  animal; rigorous to someone who does. Vector-clean edges, gallery
  quality.
- Composition fits inside a circle (icon-safe) and squares off cleanly
  for apparel. Generous negative space.

## SPECIES PROMPTS (one per follow-up)

**1 · Woolly Mammoth (anchor).**
Subject: one woolly mammoth in profile facing left. It must read as a
WOOLLY mammoth, not a generic elephant. Hold all of this distinctive
anatomy: a tall, slightly-peaked head dome as the highest point of the
animal; SMALL cold-adapted ears (a fraction of an elephant's), close to
the head; a short neck so the head sits tight to a pronounced shoulder
hump; a back that slopes steeply down to smaller, lower hindquarters;
long ivory tusks curved as a logarithmic spiral, sweeping out, down,
then curling inward and up toward each other; trunk curling forward and
down; a continuous fur 'skirt' hanging along the belly and flanks in an
even, self-similar rhythm, with a subtle suggestion of fur over the head
dome; a short tail with a small tuft. Eye: one solid amber dot (#E8B960),
small, set high and forward where head fur meets face. Construct the mass
from two overlapping circles (head dome + shoulder). Champagne line,
ivory tusks. Unmistakable at arm's length and at 3 cm. (Hero/large-format
only, optional: the two finger-like tips at the trunk's end.)

**2 · Smilodon (sabertooth).**
Subject: one Smilodon, seated or standing in calm profile, facing left.
Heavy front, round ear, the two long canines present but rendered as
clean confident lines, not menacing. Dignity over ferocity. Body from a
large circle (chest) and a smaller one (head). Champagne line, ivory
canines, amber inner-ear accent.

**3 · Glyptodon.**
Subject: one Glyptodon, profile facing left. The domed carapace is the
hero: a near-perfect half-circle shell with a subtle geometric tessellation
(hexagons or a radial pattern) implied by a few lines, not filled in.
Small head and stubby legs beneath, short armored tail. Make the dome a
deliberate circle study. Champagne shell line, amber radial accent, ivory
underbody.

**4 · Giant Ground Sloth (Megatherium).**
Subject: one giant ground sloth, seated upright on haunches and tail,
gentle profile/3-4 facing left, reaching one arm slightly. Big soft
curved claws, small head, heavy relaxed body. Posture reads slow and
kind. Body from stacked circles (haunch, belly, chest, head). Champagne
line, ivory claws, one amber eye.

**5 · Megaloceros (stretch, walk phase).**
Subject: one Irish elk, profile facing left, small lean body beneath
enormous palmate antlers. Build the antlers as a symmetrical golden-ratio
fan, the visual spectacle of the piece. Amber antlers, champagne body,
ivory eye.

## BLUEPRINT VARIANT (gallery print — run on the winner)
Take the finished [species] line illustration and overlay its
construction geometry: the circles, arcs, golden-ratio guides, and
center axes used to build it, drawn as thin ivory guide lines behind the
champagne animal, like an architect's or naturalist's construction
drawing. Add small unobtrusive tick marks. Still no text. The result
should look like the blueprint of a sacred form. This is the "sophistication
on second look" layer.

## Selection rubric (score 1–5, keep ≥21/25)
1. **Lovable at a glance** to someone who has no idea what it is.
2. **Sophisticated on second look** — line confidence, proportion, the
   geometry holds up.
3. **Species reads instantly** in silhouette (squint test on a phone).
4. **Brand discipline** — 4 colors held, works on obsidian AND ivory,
   sits inside a circle.
5. **Subtraction (Rams 10)** — nothing left to remove without loss.

Reject anything that scores 5 on "cute" but ≤3 on "sophisticated." The
whole brief lives or dies on holding both at once.

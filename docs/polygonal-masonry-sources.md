# Polygonal masonry catalog sources

Added 2026-11-18. The feature records walls with fitted, many-sided block faces. A site may also contain other masonry styles.

| Site | Country | Wall or masonry examined |
|---|---|---|
| Machu Picchu | Peru | Terrace retaining walls. See the engineering study’s Figure 11 and printed pp. 421–423. [Documentation](https://www.machupicchu.gob.pe/documents/2020/12_diciembre/MACHUPICCHU-INVESTIGACIONES-INTERDISCIPLINARIAS-TOMO-I.pdf) |
| Tipón | Peru | Pukará Alto platform inside the archaeological park. The restoration team describes cushion-faced polygonal work. [Documentation](https://www.culturacusco.gob.pe/noticia/patrimonio-cultural/cultura-cusco-concluyo-restauracion-de-sector-arqueologico-de-pukara-alto-tipon/) |
| Chucuito (Inca Uyo) | Peru | Two courses of worked polygonal blocks in the enclosure wall. [Documentation](https://consultasenlinea.mincetur.gob.pe/fichaInventario/index.aspx?cod_Ficha=425) |
| Killarumiyoq | Peru | The field inventory identifies polygonal cushion-faced blocks in the zigzag structures (p. 57). [Documentation](https://repositorio.uap.edu.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12990/9497/Tesis_Potencialidad_Ecotur%C3%ADstica_Comunidad.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1) |
| Qenqo | Peru | Southern terraces at Q’enqo Grande (archaeological park plan). [Documentation](https://transparencia.cultura.gob.pe/sites/default/files/transparencia/2024/10/resoluciones-ministeriales/rmndeg380-2024-mcanexopan2024-2030parte1.pdf) |
| Cusilluchayoc | Peru | Retaining wall beside the niches at Kusilluchayoq. [Documentation](https://transparencia.cultura.gob.pe/sites/default/files/transparencia/2024/10/resoluciones-ministeriales/rmndeg380-2024-mcanexopan2024-2030parte1.pdf) |
| Tambomachay | Peru | Retaining walls in the fountains sector and walling in the terraced sector. [Documentation](https://transparencia.cultura.gob.pe/sites/default/files/transparencia/2024/10/resoluciones-ministeriales/rmndeg380-2024-mcanexopan2024-2030parte1.pdf) |
| Inkilltambo | Peru | Stair, terrace and room walls in the park survey. [Documentation](https://transparencia.cultura.gob.pe/sites/default/files/transparencia/2024/10/resoluciones-ministeriales/rmndeg380-2024-mcanexopan2024-2030parte1.pdf) |
| Teteqaqa (Cusco) | Peru | The municipal survey documents polygonal limestone retaining walls laid with mud mortar (pp. 18–19). [Documentation](https://catalogacion.cusco.gob.pe/assets/pdf/ambientes-urbanos/plazas-miradores/AMBIENTE%20URBANO%20TETEQAQA.pdf) |
| Vilcashuaman | Peru | The excavation report identifies polygonal masonry on the ushnu terrace and in the palace sector. [Documentation](https://doi.org/10.18800/boletindearqueologiapucp.200201.005) |
| Amaru Markawasi | Peru | The Salón Punku survey identifies cushion-faced polygonal walling at the southern rock-face enclosures (printed pp. 113–114). [Documentation](https://transparencia.cultura.gob.pe/sites/default/files/transparencia/2024/10/resoluciones-ministeriales/rmndeg380-2024-mcanexopan2024-2030parte1.pdf) |
| Raqchi (Temple of Wiracocha) | Peru | Cusco’s culture ministry identifies finely worked polygonal masonry at the base of the Temple of Wiracocha’s central wall. [Documentation](https://www.culturacusco.gob.pe/parques/) |
| Zone X (Cheqtaqaqa) | Peru | The Lanlacuyoc inventory records fitted polygonal enclosure and retaining walls at the mapped location (printed pp. 114–115). [Documentation](https://transparencia.cultura.gob.pe/sites/default/files/transparencia/2024/10/resoluciones-ministeriales/rmndeg380-2024-mcanexopan2024-2030parte1.pdf) |
| Sillustani | Peru | The restoration survey distinguishes polygonal masonry on some chullpas from other wall types (pp. 54–55). [Documentation](https://es.scribd.com/document/315447924/Tesis-Puesta-en-Valor-y-Tecnicas-de-Restauracion) |
| Cutimbo | Peru | The excavation report identifies cushion-faced polygonal andesite in the facing of Chullpa 2 (figure 6). [Documentation](https://www.scielo.cl/scielo.php?pid=S0717-73562006000100010&script=sci_arttext) |
| Chinchero | Peru | The Q’entepata field report documents fitted polygonal limestone. The site museum study identifies cushion-faced polygonal walls in an excavation trench. [Documentation](https://repositorio.unsaac.edu.pe/server/api/core/bitstreams/21868b68-c33b-45cf-a203-6877f30dfca5/content) |
| Mycenae | Greece | A British School at Athens excavation report discusses polygonal sections of the citadel’s fortifications. [Documentation](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/annual-of-the-british-school-at-athens/article/abs/excavation-of-the-greek-temples-at-mycenae-by-the-british-school-at-athens/04AC4947FC8A1F312B02D6A8DC6FA2E9) |
| Eleutherai Fortress | Greece | The Mazi Archaeological Project identified retaining walls built in polygonal masonry in the first phase. [Documentation](https://www.antikekunst.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/AK_60-2017_10_Fachard_web.pdf) |
| Xanthos | Türkiye | The field study identifies polygonal masonry in the Lycian Acropolis fortifications. [Documentation](https://www.persee.fr/doc/anatv_1013-9559_1993_act_3_1_889?pageId=T1_36) |
| Termessos | Türkiye | The hydraulic field survey identifies polygonal masonry supporting the stone vault of Cistern D3 (figure 5). [Documentation](https://www.academia.edu/26570233/Termessos_taki_Su_Yap%C4%B1lar%C4%B1_Ara%C5%9Ft%C4%B1rmas%C4%B1_2010_Research_on_the_Hydraulic_Developments_of_Termessos_in_2010) |
| Hattusa | Türkiye | Architectural field study illustrates carefully fitted irregular blocks in the gates and temples (pp. 255–257). [Documentation](https://www.arct.cam.ac.uk/system/files/documents/vol-1-251-266-bachmann.pdf) |
| Alaca Höyük | Türkiye | Field study documents closely joined irregular blocks in Hittite masonry at Alaca Höyük (pp. 255–257). [Documentation](https://www.arct.cam.ac.uk/system/files/documents/vol-1-251-266-bachmann.pdf) |
| Qasr el Sagha | Egypt | Site expedition photographs show the dressed, interlocking block faces in the temple walls. [Documentation](https://www.sabrizain.org/egypt/ISIDA%20Project/Project%20Files/isida-project.ucoz.com/egypt_dec_2013/qasr_el_sagha.htm) |
| Acropolis of Circeii | Italy | The park identifies polygonal limestone masonry in the acropolis enceinte. [Documentation](https://www.parcocirceo.it/pun_dettaglio.php?id_pun=2034) |
| Temple of Isis (Fondi) | Italy | Lazio Parks describes the polygonal dry-laid terrace wall. [Documentation](https://www.parchilazio.it/schede-682-il_tempio_di_iside_villa_placitelli_fondi) |
| Porta Sanguinaria (Ferentino) | Italy | Ferentino documents polygonal lower walling at the gate. [Documentation](https://comune.ferentino.fr.it/it/page/porta-sanguinaria) |
| Cefalù Polygonal Walls | Italy | The regional archaeological publication documents polygonal walling at the Temple of Diana. [Documentation](https://www2.regione.sicilia.it/beniculturali/dirbenicult/info/pubblicazioni/Memorie_dalla_terra/Memorie_dalla_terra.pdf) |
| Orto della Regina | Italy | The wall survey identifies dry-laid polygonal trachyte masonry. [Documentation](https://www.academia.edu/35354957/Scheda_Tecnica_Orto_della_Regina_Roccamonfina_CE_) |
| Persepolis | Iran | The architectural study identifies later polygonal terrace masonry beside the entrance stairway (figure 4C). [Documentation](https://www.academia.edu/94685544/Achaemenid_Building_Technology) |
| Monte Pallano | Italy | Abruzzo’s archaeological authority documents a restored stretch of the ancient polygonal wall circuit. [Documentation](https://sabapchpe.cultura.gov.it/soprintendenza/aree-tematiche/patrimonio-archeologico/monte-pallano/) |
| Vetulonia | Italy | The archaeological museum identifies the surviving wall of the Arce as polygonal masonry. [Documentation](https://www.museoisidorofalchi.it/aree-archeologiche-nazionali-di-vetulonia/) |
| Nuraghe Corbos | Italy | The regional documentation describes polygonal lower courses of the basalt tower. [Documentation](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/it/esplora/nuraghe-corbos) |
| Hatun Rumiyoc (Twelve-Angled Stone) | Peru | Follow the joints around the Twelve-Angled Stone and look for the other many-sided blocks beside it. [Documentation](https://consultasenlinea.mincetur.gob.pe/fichaInventario/index.aspx?cod_Ficha=2230) |
| Qespihuanka (Palace of Huayna Cápac) | Peru | Compare the many-sided stones in the lower wall with the rougher courses above. [Documentation](https://consultasenlinea.mincetur.gob.pe/fichaInventario/index.aspx?cod_Ficha=12387) |
| Ahu Vinapu (Rapa Nui) | Chile | Look at the joints on the seaward wall of Ahu Tahira, where the dressed slabs face the ocean. [Documentation](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10085198/1/08_Hamilton_Rapa_Nui%2810%29.pdf) |
| Shuri Castle (Okinawa) | Japan | Trace the irregular stone joints in the outer enclosures and compare them with the more regular stone courses nearby. [Documentation](https://oki-park.jp/userfiles/files/shurijo/digital_museum/data/akahon_03.pdf) |

# Stone Riddles channel audit, 24 October 2026

Inventory: 84 regular uploads and 80 Shorts from the channel’s public tabs. The companion JSON lists every upload and its disposition. This audit used public descriptions, selected original footage, the Italy documentary’s transcript and frame samples, and independent site records. It does not claim that every frame of every upload was watched.

## Published changes

- 18 new monument locations, including both Uore monuments, Orto della Regina, Porta Aurea, Ponte Alto, Grotte di Torri, Amelia, Circeii, Kazarma Bridge and five locations named in members-only clips.
- Corrected the existing Rusellae coordinates to the Roselle archaeological park, retaining the canonical name and all previous film associations.
- 15 timestamped associations for the freely accessible Italy documentary. These cover 13 principal locations plus the separate Porta Saracena and Porta Foca gateways.
- Members-only Shorts are explicitly labelled source references on site pages, never added as freely playable walkthroughs. Porta Foca also has an accessible documentary timestamp.
- Existing films, creators, photographs and licences preserved.

## Coordinate notes

- Uore spring: Sardinian regional landscape inventory record 968, X 1480744 / Y 4451506, converted from Gauss-Boaga west (EPSG:3003) to WGS84. Coordinates are indicative inventory coordinates, not a visitor-access guarantee.
- Caslasc: archaeological survey LV95 E 2710250 / N 1139030, converted through the Swiss federal geodesy service. Dating remains uncertain; no prehistoric attribution invented.
- Orto della Regina: Wikidata monument point cross-checked against the named enclosure and CAI route documentation. The field-map point farther north was not substituted without confirmation.
- Santa Severa: castle/museum wall complex, not the separate Etruscan sanctuary farther along the coast.
- Individual references are stored with each site. The privately maintained Megalithic Minutiae map, credited in the Terracina film, supplied location leads; official archaeological or municipal sources were used where available for monument identity and interpretation.

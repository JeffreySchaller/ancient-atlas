# Sardinia and Corsica map review, 24 October 2026

Adds 19 mapped places, alongside the two Sardinian places and four local films from the local-creator review. Corsica previously had Filitosa only. Santa Cristina was not previously mapped.

## Sources and scope

The source ledger is `data/island-map-review.json`. Each new place includes a public archaeological or regional tourism reference. Coordinates were checked by named site, rather than taking the first search result. Tappa uses the on-site location 41.552272, 9.231043. The era filter uses representative approximate years, not dates for every phase of a monument. Descriptions concern visible architecture and documented monument types, without adopting filmmakers' speculative historical interpretations.

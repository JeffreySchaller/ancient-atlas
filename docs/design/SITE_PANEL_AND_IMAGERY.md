# Video-first site panels

Lead with the place and a specific reason to look. Use the stable action label “Watch here” so visitors know what clicking does. Editorial variety belongs in the introductory sentence, not in changing playback terminology.

`data/site-intros.json` holds one editable opening per site. Initial entries use the existing description's opening sentence; Oga, Cosa, Tipón, Sacsayhuamán, Moray, Ollantaytambo and Göbekli Tepe have tailored observation invitations. These are editorial introductions, not new evidence or video transcripts. Future additions should get a concise, source-supported reason to watch rather than a generic “Explore…” sentence.

Every film remains present, in its existing order, with original title, named creator, direct channel link and timestamp. No-film sites retain photographs and details. No automatic first-party priority.

## Characteristics

`data/site-character-icons.json` defines the initial labeled symbols: large upright blocks for megalithic stonework, irregular interlocking blocks for polygonal walls, and paired faces with a narrow seam for precision joinery. The latter two use existing site criteria only. Open interpretations retain “look closer” wording. Do not infer those characteristics from a creator title or apply them universally to a category. No marker icons were replaced in this release.

## Thumbnail direction

This release keeps original YouTube thumbnails and gives them more visual space. No film frames were extracted, generated or substituted. A future editorial image field should retain image source, credit, usage permission/license and a fallback to the creator thumbnail. Review a few films first: choose a clear, truthful view of the site's distinguishing feature, keep the original title and creator attribution, and label any separate Atlas editorial image appropriately. Do not automatically invent dramatic reconstructions or silently replace a creator's imagery at scale.

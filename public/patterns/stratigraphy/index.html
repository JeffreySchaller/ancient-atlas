What the pattern is, and what it is not: the argument in full

This is the most contested thing the Atlas tracks, and the tag means a dispute exists, not that it has been settled. Geological dating of a monument is genuinely hard: erosion rates vary, fill gets redeposited, and a buried course can mean an older phase or a later repair.

So these are collected as the argument rather than the answer. Where a claim is disputed in the literature, that dispute is the point of the page.

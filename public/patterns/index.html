Patterns

The same idea, in places that never met.

The Atlas is normally sorted by where things are. This shelf sorts it by how they were made — seven engineering signatures tracked across 618 sites, each with the comparative studies that argue it. Sort by country and these never appear together. Sort by method and they are obviously one idea.

No. 01 · Machining

Tool marks that repeat on four continents.

Striations, bore holes, saw kerfs and flat-planed faces that look mechanical — turning up in cultures separated by oceans and thousands of years, in stone their published toolkits were not supposed to cut.

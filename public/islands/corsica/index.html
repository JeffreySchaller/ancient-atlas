Across the Strait of Bonifacio

Two islands. Related questions.

Corsica lies directly north of Sardinia. Comparing individual features helps the eye; each monument still needs its own history.

Towers and stonework

Torre and nuraghi

Corsican torre and Sardinian nuraghi share circular plans and corbelled stone roofing. The size and development of their complexes differ. Compare rooms, entrances and walls before drawing conclusions about purpose.

Megalithic burial

Large stones, different tombs

Funtanaccia is a dolmen with upright supports and a capstone. Sardinia’s Giants’ Tombs have their own distinctive form. The comparison concerns large-stone construction and collective burial.

What this survey has not matched

Keep the differences visible

No close Corsican counterpart to Santa Cristina’s built sacred well or Sardinia’s decorated Domus de Janas was verified in this image survey. Natural granite shelters are a different feature.

Site details
PyramidEgypt

Old Kingdom pyramid complex on the west bank of the Nile between Giza and Saqqara, used as the royal necropolis of the 5th Dynasty (c. 2494-2345 BCE). Contains the pyramids of Sahure, Neferirkare, Neferefre, and Niuserre, plus the sun temples of Userkaf and Niuserre. Independent researchers including UnchartedX have documented precision-machined granite artifacts including paired stone boxes with inner/outer precision tolerances that mainstream archaeology has not fully explained. The Abu Sir Papyri (found here in the 1890s) are among the oldest surviving administrative documents from ancient Egypt.

Aksum Obelisks

MegalithicAfricaEthiopiac. 200 CEAksumite

Giant stelae up to 33m tall carved from single granite blocks. The largest (fallen) weighs 520 tons. Aksumite civilization was one of the four great powers of the ancient world.

Site details
PyramidCentral AmericaMexico

Second-largest pyramid at Teotihuacan, aligned with the symmetry axis of the city and the contour of the sacred Mount Tlaloc behind it. 43 m tall. Built in seven construction phases between 100 BCE and 350 CE. Recent excavations have revealed elaborate offerings and burials beneath the pyramid.

19.6981°, -98.8439°

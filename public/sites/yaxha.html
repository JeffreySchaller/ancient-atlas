Yaxha

City / RuinsCentral America

Maya city on the shore of Lake Yaxha in Guatemala's Petén region. Featured astronomical alignments with both the cardinal directions and solstice sunrises. Multiple plazas, ball courts, and a network of causeways. Active from the Late Preclassic through Terminal Classic periods.

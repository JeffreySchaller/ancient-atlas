Coba

City / RuinsCentral America

Maya city in the Yucatán jungle with the tallest pyramid in the northern Maya lowlands (Nohoch Mul, 42 m). Connected to other Maya cities by an extensive network of raised limestone causeways (sacbeob), some 100+ km long. Active 200-1100 CE.

Kalavantin Durg

MegalithicAsia

Pinnacle fortress in Maharashtra with a stairway cut directly into vertical rock face, ascending to the summit. Origin and purpose disputed — neither dating nor builders confirmed. Local mythology dates it to ancient Hindu period; alt-archaeology proposes far older origins.

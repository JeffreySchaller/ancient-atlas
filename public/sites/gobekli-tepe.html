Göbekli Tepe

MegalithicTürkiyec. 9,600 BCEPre-Pottery Neolithic

The world's oldest known monumental sanctuary, dating to ~9600 BCE. Massive T-shaped pillars carved with animal reliefs predate agriculture and permanent settlement.

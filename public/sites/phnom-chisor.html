Phnom Chisor

TempleAsia

11th-century hilltop Hindu temple in Takeo Province, southern Cambodia, dedicated to Shiva. Built by Suryavarman I. Unique east-facing layout with an elaborate processional staircase. 503 steps lead to the summit.

Barabar Caves

Rock-CutAsiaIndia

India's oldest surviving rock-cut caves, commissioned by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE for the Ajivika ascetic sect. Mirror-polished granite interiors achieve acoustic resonance precision not replicated elsewhere in ancient stonework.

25.0044°, 85.0617°

Naneghat

Rock-CutAsiaIndia

Ancient mountain pass cut through the Western Ghats in Maharashtra, used as a trade route by the Satavahanas (~200 BCE). Notable for a giant carved stone jar of disputed function, plus an inscription on the cave wall documenting royal grants.

19.2972°, 73.6694°

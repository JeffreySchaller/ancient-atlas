Loughcrew

MegalithicEuropeIreland

Complex of approximately thirty passage tombs spread across the Loughcrew hills (Slieve na Calliagh — 'mountain of the hag') in County Meath, Ireland. The cairns are conventionally dated c. 3300 BCE, contemporary with Newgrange and Knowth. Cairn T preserves the most spectacular megalithic art in Ireland: deeply carved concentric circles, sunburst motifs, and abstract symbols on multiple chamber stones. The cairn's passage is aligned on sunrise at the spring and autumn equinoxes — the rising sun illuminates the rear chamber and specific carvings in sequence. Hugh Newman documents the alignments and the Winnemucca petroglyph correspondence.

Site details
TempleAsiaCambodia

Buddhist temple on an artificial island in the center of the Jayatataka Baray at Angkor, built by Jayavarman VII in the late 12th century. Designed to mimic the sacred Anavatapta lake of Buddhist cosmology, with four connected pools radiating from a central circular pond.

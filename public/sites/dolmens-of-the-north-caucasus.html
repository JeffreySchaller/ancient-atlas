Site details
MegalithicEuropeRussia

Bronze Age megalithic tomb complex spanning the Krasnodar and Adygea regions near the Black Sea. Roughly 3,000 documented dolmens, constructed from precisely cut stone slabs weighing up to 20 tons, many featuring carved portholes and ornamental relief work. Carbon-dated to the 3rd-2nd millennium BCE. The masonry precision rivals Western European megalithic traditions and predates them in several places.

44.5500°, 38.0833°

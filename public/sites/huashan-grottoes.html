Huashan Grottoes

UndergroundAsiaChina

36 hand-carved sandstone caverns near the Yellow River, similar in technique to Longyou Caves. Vast interiors with millions of cubic feet of stone removed, no historical record of construction, no debris pile in the surrounding landscape. Many chambers remain unexplored.

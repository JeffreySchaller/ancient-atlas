Puma Punku

MegalithicSouth AmericaBoliviac. 500 CETiwanaku

Part of the Tiwanaku complex. H-shaped diorite blocks with machine-like precision: identical angles, smooth surfaces, and interlocking joints that seem impossible for the era.

-16.5617°, -68.6800°

Look Closer — this site carries open questions: stones fitted without mortar, almost no gap; tool marks suggesting advanced machining; stone harder than steel; geometry beyond period explanation.

Vishap Stones

MegalithicMiddle EastArmeniac. 2,500 BCEBronze Age Armenian

'Dragon stones' of the Armenian highlands: tall basalt menhirs carved as stylized fish or bull-horn forms, often near water sources at altitudes above 2,000m. Dated to ~3rd–2nd millennium BCE. Over 150 examples documented. The earliest known association of monumental stone with water-deity worship in Western Asia.

Site details
Rock-CutAsiaIndiac. 600 CEIndian rock-cut / Hindu-Buddhist-Jain

34 caves spanning Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain traditions. The Kailasa temple (Cave 16) is the largest monolithic rock excavation in the world, carved top-down from a single basalt cliff.

20.0258°, 75.1792°

Monte d'Accoddi

TempleEuropeItalyc. 3,500 BCEPre-Nuragic Sardinian

A raised prehistoric altar-platform approached by a long ramp near Sassari. Excavations revealed successive building phases, with standing stones, large slabs and settlement remains around the monument.

40.7911°, 8.4489°

Yonaguni Monument

MegalithicAsiaJapan

Submerged rock formation off Japan with apparent terraces, right angles, and carved features. Debated: natural geology or a 10,000-year-old man-made structure lost to rising seas.

24.4350°, 122.9342°

Look Closer — this site carries open questions: stones heavier than the period's tools could lift; stones fitted without mortar, almost no gap; geometry beyond period explanation. See scale across the Atlas →

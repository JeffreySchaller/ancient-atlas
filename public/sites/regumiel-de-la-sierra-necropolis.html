Regumiel de la Sierra Necropolis

Tomb / BurialEuropeSpainc. 1000 CEEarly medieval Iberian

An extensive early medieval cemetery occupies the sandstone outcrop around the church of San Adrián Mártir. Roughly a hundred graves are documented, with further burials likely beneath later church alterations and access steps. This is the village church necropolis, distinct from nearby Revenga.

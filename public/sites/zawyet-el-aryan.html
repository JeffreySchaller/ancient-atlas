Zawyet el-Aryan

PyramidEgyptc. 2,650 BCEOld Kingdom Egyptian

Two unfinished pyramids of the 3rd or 4th Dynasty: the Layer Pyramid (Khaba) and the Unfinished Pyramid (sometimes attributed to Baka). The latter's burial pit contains a single sealed granite vessel of unknown function. Closed to the public for nearly a century; remains poorly excavated.

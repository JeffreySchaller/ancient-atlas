Cosa

City / RuinsEuropeItalyc. 273 BCEEtruscan then Roman

Roman colony on the Tyrrhenian coast of Tuscany, founded 273 BCE on a pre-existing megalithic substrate. The polygonal foundation walls and acropolis platform pre-date the Roman period. Etruscan or pre-Etruscan origin contested.

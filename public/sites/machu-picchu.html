Machu Picchu

City / RuinsSouth AmericaPeruc. 1450 CEInca

Inca citadel at 2,430m in the Andes, built ~1450 CE. Precisely cut granite blocks fitted without mortar. Rediscovered by Hiram Bingham in 1911.

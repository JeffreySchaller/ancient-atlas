Porta Saracena (Segni)

MegalithicEuropeItalyc. 500 BCEAncient Italy

A monumental gateway in the polygonal stone walls of ancient Segni. Large fitted blocks frame the passage through the wall. This pin marks the gate itself, separately from the broader Segni town site.

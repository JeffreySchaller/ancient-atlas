Chacchoben

City / RuinsCentral America

Maya ceremonial center in southern Quintana Roo, Mexico. Multiple pyramids and monuments in the Petén style. Active c. 200 BCE to 700 CE. Less well-known than Tulum or Chichen Itza, with intact pyramids still surrounded by jungle.

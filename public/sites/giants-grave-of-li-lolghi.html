Giants' Grave of Li Lolghi

Tomb / BurialEuropeItalyc. 1,600 BCENuragic

A long collective burial monument near Arzachena, with a curved forecourt of upright slabs and a tall central stele. A small opening at the stele’s base leads toward the burial corridor.

41.0667°, 9.3337°

Site reference: Sardegna Turismo

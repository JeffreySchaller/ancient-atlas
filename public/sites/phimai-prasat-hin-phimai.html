Phimai (Prasat Hin Phimai)

TempleAsiaThailand

Largest Khmer sandstone temple complex in Thailand, located in Nakhon Ratchasima province. The architectural prototype for Angkor Wat. Built primarily during the reign of Jayavarman VI (late 11th c.) on the foundations of an earlier Mahayana Buddhist sanctuary, with massive sandstone blocks fitted to precision tolerances comparable to the Cuzco Inca walls. The site's central prang anchored the northern terminus of the Khmer royal road that ran south to Angkor. Independent investigators including Praveen Mohan have documented evidence of repeating tool-mark signatures that mainstream archaeology attributes to iron chisels but that some researchers argue are too uniform for hand work.

Cusilluchayoc

Rock-CutSouth AmericaPeruc. 1450 CEInca

Complex of carved limestone rocks, caves and corridors about 500 m east of Qenqo — the 'Temple of the Monkeys.' Zoomorphic relief figures read as monkeys, serpents and pumas accompany carved walls and stone water channels; Spanish extirpators of idolatry chiseled the heads off the figures, leaving the popular 'monkey' identification an open interpretive question. Offerings are still left at a stone locals call the heart of Pachamama.

Nan Madol

MegalithicPacificMicronesia

Artificial island city built on a coral reef off Pohnpei, Micronesia. 92 islands connected by canals, built from an estimated 750,000 tons of basalt columns. Called the 'Venice of the Pacific.'

6.8444°, 158.3356°

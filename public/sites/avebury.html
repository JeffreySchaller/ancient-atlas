Avebury

MegalithicEuropeUnited Kingdomc. 2,600 BCEMegalithic / Wessex

Largest stone circle in the world by diameter (420m), enclosing a village. Part of a vast Neolithic landscape including Silbury Hill and the West Kennet Long Barrow.

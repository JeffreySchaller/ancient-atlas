Derinkuyu Underground City

UndergroundTürkiyec. 700 BCEPhrygian / Byzantine

Massive underground city extending 60m deep with 18 levels. Could shelter 20,000 people with livestock and food stores. Features ventilation shafts, wells, and a cruciform church on the lowest level.

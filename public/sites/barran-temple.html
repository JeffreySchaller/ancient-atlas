Barran Temple

TempleMiddle EastYemen

Sabaean temple complex near Marib, Yemen, also dedicated to the moon god Almaqah. Smaller than nearby Awwam but featuring the same precision-cut limestone masonry tradition with six monumental pillars still standing. Dated c. 7th century BCE.

15.4117°, 45.3522°

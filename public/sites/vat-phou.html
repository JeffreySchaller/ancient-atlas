Vat Phou

TempleAsiaLaos

Pre-Angkorian Khmer temple complex in southern Laos, dating to the 5th century CE. Built on a holy mountain. UNESCO World Heritage. Stylistic precursor to Angkor Wat. Megalithic foundations remain unexplained.

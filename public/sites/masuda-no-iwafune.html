Masuda no Iwafune

MegalithicAsiaJapan

The 'Stone Ship of Masuda' in Asuka, Nara. An 800-ton boat-shaped megalith on a hilltop, precisely carved with grooves and twin square holes. Origin and purpose unknown. Pre-dates the Asuka period's recorded histories.

Guyaju Caves

Rock-CutAsia

Largest cliff dwelling complex in China, ~50 miles northwest of Beijing. 117 caves and 350 chambers carved from solid granite across 25 acres. Discovered 1984. No historical record of builders. Architectural sophistication (staircases, drainage, stone furniture) suggests advanced engineering.

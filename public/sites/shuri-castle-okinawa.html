Site details
City / RuinsAsiaJapan

This former Ryukyu royal castle in Naha has limestone retaining and enclosure walls. The restoration record documents aikata-zumi, a technique using irregular cut stones fitted to one another, in sections of the outer walls. The visible complex includes restored fabric.

26.2170°, 127.7195°

Site reference: Okinawa park restoration record, p. 123 · UNESCO Ryukyu World Heritage nomination · Wikimedia Commons, geolocated Shuri Castle photograph

Bakong

TempleAsiaCambodiac. 881 CEKhmer Empire (early)

First state temple of the Khmer Empire (~881 CE), in the Roluos group near Siem Reap. The earliest large pyramid-temple in Khmer architecture, foreshadowing the form of Angkor Wat. Sandstone over laterite, surrounded by a moat.

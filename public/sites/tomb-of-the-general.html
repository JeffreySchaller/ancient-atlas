Site details
PyramidAsiaChina

Stepped pyramid in Ji'an, Jilin Province. Officially a Goguryeo-era tomb (~5th century CE), but the seven-tier granite block construction parallels Mesoamerican pyramid forms and may predate the Goguryeo Kingdom that reused it as a burial monument.

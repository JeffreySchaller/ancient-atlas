Araghju (Corsica)

MegalithicEuropeFrancec. 1,500 BCEBronze Age Corsican

A Bronze Age hilltop enclosure above the Porto-Vecchio plain. Massive granite walls surround inner rooms and passages, with a substantial lintel over the main entrance.

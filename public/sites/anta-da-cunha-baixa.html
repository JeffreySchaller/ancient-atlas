Anta da Cunha Baixa

Tomb / BurialEuropePortugalc. 3,000 BCELate Neolithic / Chalcolithic Iberian

A large capstone covers the granite chamber of this passage grave near Cunha Baixa in Mangualde. Its long corridor retains standing slabs, and investigations recorded painted and engraved decoration. Portugal’s heritage register dates construction between the late fourth and mid-third millennia BCE, with later revisitation.

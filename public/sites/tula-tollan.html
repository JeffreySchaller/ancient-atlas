Tula (Tollan)

City / RuinsCentral America

Capital of the Toltec civilization in Hidalgo, central Mexico, occupied c. 900-1150 CE. Famous for the 4.6 m tall basalt Atlantean warrior columns atop Pyramid B. Archaic Lens documents tubular drill marks and surface machining patterns on stones at the site that are difficult to reconcile with the assumed Toltec toolkit. The Toltecs claimed cultural descent from earlier Teotihuacán (which they called Tollan-Teotihuacán) and their architectural vocabulary echoes both Teotihuacán and the Maya cities further south, suggesting a deeper continuity than the conventional cultural breaks acknowledge.

20.0667°, -99.3406°

Look Closer — this site carries open questions: stones fitted without mortar, almost no gap; tool marks suggesting advanced machining; stones heavier than the period's tools could lift.

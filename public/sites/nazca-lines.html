Nazca Lines

GeoglyphSouth AmericaPeruc. 100 CENazca

Hundreds of enormous geoglyphs etched into the desert, visible only from altitude. Figures include a hummingbird, spider, and monkey spanning up to 370m. Purpose remains debated.

-14.7350°, -75.1300°

Banteay Srei

TempleAsiaCambodiac. 967 CEKhmer Empire

'Citadel of the Women' (967 CE), 25 km northeast of Angkor. Built from pink sandstone with exceptionally fine relief carvings — Cambodia's most exquisitely detailed temple. Modest size, extraordinary execution.

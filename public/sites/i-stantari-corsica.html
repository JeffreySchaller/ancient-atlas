I Stantari (Corsica)

MegalithicEuropeFrancec. 1,500 BCEPrehistoric Corsican

Standing-stone alignments on the Cauria plateau, including human-shaped statue-menhirs with carved weapons and other details. The site forms part of a larger prehistoric landscape with Renaghju and Funtanaccia.

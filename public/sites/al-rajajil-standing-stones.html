Al Rajajil Standing Stones

MegalithicMiddle EastSaudi Arabiac. 4,000 BCEPre-Islamic Arabian (Neolithic)

'The Standing Men of Saudi Arabia.' Over 50 clusters of standing sandstone slabs in the northern Saudi desert, some over 3m tall, dated to ~4,000 BCE. The function is unclear. The site is contemporary with Carnac, on a continent and culture with no known megalithic tradition.

Porta Aurea (Castellonorato)

MegalithicEuropeItalyAncient Italy

Monumental stone gateway and wall remains at Palombara near Castellonorato, Formia. The archaeological site received a cultural-interest declaration in 2026. It is on private land; access requires the owner’s permission.

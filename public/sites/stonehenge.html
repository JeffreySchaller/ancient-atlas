Stonehenge

MegalithicEuropeUnited Kingdomc. 2,500 BCEMegalithic / Wessex

The world's most famous stone circle, built in stages from ~3000-2000 BCE. Bluestones transported 250km from Wales. Aligned to summer solstice sunrise and winter solstice sunset.

51.1789°, -1.8262°

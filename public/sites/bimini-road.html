Bimini Road

MegalithicCentral AmericaBahamasc. 10,000 BCEDisputed / pre-Holocene?

Underwater rock formation off North Bimini in the Bahamas, ~6m deep. Linear strip of rectangular megalithic blocks ~800m long. Discovered 1968. Geological versus man-made origin debated for over 50 years. Edgar Cayce's predicted 'edge of Atlantis' site.

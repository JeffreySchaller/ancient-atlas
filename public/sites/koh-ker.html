Site details
PyramidAsiaCambodiac. 928 CEKhmer Empire (or older?)

20,000-acre megalithic complex 100 km NE of Angkor, anchored by a solid stone seven-tier pyramid (Prasat Thom). Officially built in 20 years by Jayavarman IV (~928 CE), with 150+ structures, 7 km man-made dam, and a 2,000-acre reservoir — the largest artificial lake in the ancient world. Polygonal stonework reminiscent of Cusco. Home to colossal lingas and the 7-ton Dancing Shiva.

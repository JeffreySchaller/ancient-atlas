Madghacen (Algerian Pyramids)

Tomb / BurialAfricaAlgeriac. 300 BCENumidian (Berber)

Numidian royal mausoleum in eastern Algeria, ~3rd century BCE. A 19m-tall stepped circular tomb with 60 Doric columns. Contemporary with the larger Berber pyramid-tomb at Tipasa. Both pre-date Roman occupation of North Africa.

Monte Baranta

MegalithicEuropeItalyc. 2,500 BCEPre-Nuragic Sardinian

A Copper Age complex above Olmedo with a massive stone enclosure, a long wall and a horseshoe-shaped structure overlooking the cliff. Nearby standing stones and settlement remains broaden the picture beyond the enclosure itself.

Site details
TempleEuropeItalyc. 1,000 BCENuragic

A descending stairway of carefully dressed basalt leads to a circular water chamber beneath a corbelled roof. The well belongs to a larger Nuragic sanctuary near Paulilatino, with a nuraghe and later religious buildings nearby.

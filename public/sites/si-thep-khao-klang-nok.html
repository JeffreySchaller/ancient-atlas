Si Thep (Khao Klang Nok)

City / RuinsAsiaThailandc. 700 CEDvaravati culture

Dvaravati-period (~600-900 CE) twin-walled city in Phetchabun, Thailand. Khao Klang Nok is a 64m-wide pyramid-platform of fired brick. UNESCO World Heritage 2023. Architectural style suggests cross-cultural Indian influence.

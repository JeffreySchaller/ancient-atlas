Angkor Wat

TempleAsiaCambodiac. 1150 CEKhmer Empire

Largest religious monument ever built (162.6 hectares). Hindu-then-Buddhist temple with towers representing Mt. Meru, surrounded by a vast moat. Built by Suryavarman II (~1150 CE).

Site details
MegalithicEuropeFrancec. 3,300 BCETorrean / megalithic Corsican

Megalithic complex on the southern French island of Corsica, occupied from ~3300 BCE. Anthropomorphic statue-menhirs depicting armed warriors, some of the earliest representations of the human figure in European megalithic art. Distinct from but contemporary with Carnac.
